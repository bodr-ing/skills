Logo, tried for real

Demo: the real run that picked the logo for The Bodring Company (bodr.ing).

The eight image-model slices, redrawn by hand as clean SVG and put in seven real places. Scroll the uses, then pick at the bottom.

My read: 06 Stamp

It holds up everywhere: the fat pink seed pocket still says “cucumber” at 16 px, on a phone and on a poster across the street, and it survives one-colour printing. Close second is 04 Bite, which is cheekier. A mix of the two (Stamp with a bite) is worth a try if you like the wink. 05 Mascot and 08 Hand-drawn look great big but fall apart small.
