A real run

How bodr.ing got its look and its mark

Bir bodring means “one cucumber” in Uzbek. The Bodring Company knew what it was: an umbrella for a few apps and games. It didn't know how it should look, so it started at step two. A new venture starts one step earlier, with YepNope.

  1. OohNah40 looks, 22 yeses

    The swipes leaned Uzbek and cultural, techy, premium and playful, with green and print. The clear noes were gritty, pastel and Y2K, and generic SaaS. Three directions came back, and Pickle press won: two-colour riso, off-register, a halftone cucumber.

    Bagel Fat One · Work Sans · DM Mono, the page you're reading.

  2. Logo Lab8 marks, 20 photos

    Eight concepts from an image model, redrawn as SVG and tried in seven places, from a 16px tab to a one-colour stamp. The four finalists went onto a Tashkent poster, a phone, a tote, a pickle jar and a shop window.

    See the whole run ↗

  3. ShippedThe slice

    bdring

    A real cucumber cross-section with a pink plate offset like a misprint. It is the favicon, and it replaces the “o” in the wordmark. The first version, a flat four-seed slice, read as a button, so it was redrawn.

    The pick and the reason went into taste.md, so the next agent designs from it instead of starting over.

Any subject · ideas in words

YepNope

Find out what someone actually wants when they can't say it yet. People can rarely describe it, but they react to a concrete idea in two seconds. A hundred small answers add up to a clear picture.

  1. The agent breaks the subject down into its smallest choices: 12 to 25 categories, 150 to 300 one-idea cards.
  2. You swipe about 100. Right is yes, left is no, up is love it, down is don't care.
  3. It reads your answers back as a profile and concrete directions you can build.

Then ask your agentHelp me figure out what game I want to make.You can also ask for"just 30 swipes""only art and story""5 directions""two rounds""in Uzbek"

Next: once you know what it is, OohNah finds how it should look.

Visual style only · looks in colour

OohNah

Find a brand's look by swiping before anyone designs a thing. Each card is a whole look: a palette, a typeface and a layout. You don't have to say why you like it.

  1. Round one maps the space with 12 looks as far apart as possible: playful to premium, light to dark, serif to mono.
  2. Round two remixes what you liked, the palette of one with the type of another, and keeps probing blind spots.
  3. It writes taste.md, with a "never" list, then builds three real directions for you to pick from.

Then ask your agentRun OohNah before we design the landing page.You can also ask for"15 swipes""only colours""5 variants""6 logo options""stop at taste.md"

Logos · tested where they live

Logo Lab

A logo is judged in use, not on a white square. Logo Lab puts every candidate where it will actually live, scores them side by side, and ends in a one-tap pick.

  1. An image model draws 8 concepts, each a different direction: minimal, sticker, angled, a bite, a mascot, a stamp, chunky, hand-drawn.
  2. The agent redraws every one as clean SVG and tries it on the site, a street poster, an app icon, an avatar, a browser tab, a sticker and in one colour.
  3. It scores them and makes photo mockups of the finalists on a poster, a phone, a tote, a jar and a shop window.
  4. You tap one. It ships as SVG, favicon and avatars, and goes into taste.md.

Then ask your agentOur logo isn't working. Run Logo Lab.You can also ask for"just 4 ideas""add a T-shirt""only the top 2 in photos""stop at the compare page"

Before: no taste.md yet? Run OohNah first, so the palette is settled before anyone draws a mark.

Get them all

They chain: YepNope finds what to build, OohNah finds how it looks and writes taste.md, Logo Lab reads that and finds the mark.
