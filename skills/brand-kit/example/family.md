# Bodring brands

The Bodring Company's family, as a worked example. Every brand has a one-word Uzbek name, a literal object behind it, and a mark that shows that object, often living inside one letter.

## Shared rules

- Uzbek names, always lowercase, in the reformed Latin alphabet (ç, ş, ö, ğ). An ASCII form for domains: açiçuk → acicuk.uz.
- Warm paper or cream grounds, flat colour, print feel. Two or three colours per brand, plus paper.
- One idea per mark, and it reads at 16px.
- Never: gritty, neon, black and gold, propaganda, pastel or Y2K, generic SaaS gradients, clip art.

## What the runs taught

- **bodring:** a flat slice read as a button; the halftone stamp still read as a cucumber at 16px and won 20 of 21.
- **açiçuk:** the mark ties back to the name (comma seeds echo the ç). Two palettes were mixed into one quieter set for big screens.
- **çaqmoq:** four concept rounds. Rounds 2 to 4 found nothing new until the one idea was named (the bolt lives only in the ç). Colour was settled after the shape, in eight colourways; navy and yellow lost for being too common.
- **All:** a swipe deck that reused another brand's demo cards was called out at once. Design every deck for its brand.
